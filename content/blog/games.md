A running index of games I recommend to friends, games I still want to try, and assorted good stuff around games.

### games I like

The ones I keep coming back to. If you ask me for a recommendation, it's probably on this list.

- [Pharaoh](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pharaoh_(video_game)>) — egyptian city builder, still holds up
- [Warcraft III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warcraft_III:_Reign_of_Chaos) — the peak of RTS, many modern genres were invented in the custom map scene of Starcraft and perfected in Warcraft III, for example Dota, Tower Defense or AOS-style games.
- [Heroes of Might and Magic III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heroes_of_Might_and_Magic_III) — turn-based strategy perfection
- [vanilla World of Warcraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_of_Warcraft) — nothing has matched the feeling since
- [Counter-Strike: Source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counter-Strike:_Source) — the only cs that I played a lot as a kid
- [Command & Conquer: Generals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Command_%26_Conquer:_Generals) — zero hour is a must
- [Grim Fandango](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grim_Fandango) — best adventure game ever made, fight me
- [Toonstruck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toonstruck) — weird 90s point-and-click cartoon adventure game
- [Broken Sword: The Shadow of the Templars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Broken_Sword:_The_Shadow_of_the_Templars) — my first adventure game, never was able to beat it as a kid
- [Minecraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minecraft) — probably the "ultimate" game, contains just about everything you can imagine if you go deeper
- [Northgard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northgard) — compact viking RTS, great for an evening
- [Frostpunk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frostpunk) — bleak survival city builder, brutal and beautiful
- [Darkstone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darkstone) — diablo-like from 1999, very underrated, iconic graphics

### wow private servers

The vanilla+ scene keeps getting better. Running private servers is the only way to actually play WoW.

- [Turtle WoW](https://turtle-wow.org/) — vanilla+ with new content, most polished of the bunch
- [Turtle forum: custom content thread](https://forum.turtle-wow.org/viewtopic.php?f=2&t=5143)
- [Project Ascension](https://ascension.gg/) — classless WoW, you pick your own spells
- [Project Epoch](https://www.project-epoch.net/) — vanilla+ taking place between classic and TBC
- [YouTube: private server overview](https://youtu.be/k-u-Iy3iOZA)
