## Broths (Sanji's Eye 4)

### Kombu and Katsuo Broth

The inosinic acid from the katsuo and the glutamine acid from the kombu combine for a great umami flavour. Keeps 2 days in the fridge, 1 week frozen. Makes ~1000 mL.

#### Ingredients

- 1200 mL water
- 1 sheet (10×10 cm) kombu
- 20 g katsuobushi (dried bonito flakes)

#### Steps

1. Soak kombu in water for at least 30 minutes.
1. Heat over medium. Remove kombu just before boiling.
1. Add katsuobushi and remove from heat. When the katsuobushi sinks, strain.

### Sanji's Chicken Stock

Use as a base for curry or pot-au-feu for doubled deliciousness. Keeps 2 days in the fridge, 1 week frozen. Makes ~2600 mL.

#### Ingredients

- 2 chicken carcasses / wings
- 3000 mL water
- 150 g vegetable scraps (green leek tops, carrot peel, ginger peel)

#### Steps

1. Rinse chicken and divide into 3 pieces.
1. Blanch in boiling water until surface turns white, then remove and clean off any blood.
1. Add water, chicken, and vegetables to a pot. Bring to a boil over medium heat, skimming froth.
1. Simmer on low 40–60 minutes. Strain out solids.

### -8- Fried Rice for Gin

*Serves 2*

#### Ingredients

- 360 g cooked rice
- 2 eggs
- 50 g corned beef
- ¼ onion, finely chopped
- 4 brown mushrooms, thinly sliced
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- ½ tsp salt
- Dash black pepper
- ½ tsp soy sauce
- Green onion to taste

#### Steps

1. Finely chop onion, thinly slice mushrooms. Chop a little green onion.
1. Heat oil in a frying pan. Stir fry half the onion and all the mushrooms. Add corned beef and stir fry. Set aside.
1. Add a little more oil, then beaten eggs. Before the egg cooks, add rice and quickly stir fry. Mix everything together. Add salt and pepper. Add remaining onion and soy sauce, mix briefly.
1. Plate and sprinkle green onion on top.

### -9- Unappetizing (Delicious) Soup

*Kombu fish soup — serves 2–3*

#### Ingredients

- 1 red snapper (~300 g), cut into large pieces
- 1 sheet (10×10 cm) kombu
- 2 turnips
- 6 stalks asparagus
- ½ lemon, sliced
- 1200 mL water
- 2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp soy sauce

#### Steps

1. Soak kombu at least 30 minutes. Blanch snapper pieces in boiling water until white, then rinse in cold water to clean off blood or dirt.
1. Cut turnips into quarters, chop leaves into small pieces. Trim the hard bottom third from asparagus.
1. Put water and cleaned fish in a pot. Bring to a boil then simmer. Add turnips (not leaves) and boil.
1. Boil 10 minutes. Add asparagus, boil 5 minutes. Add salt and soy sauce to finish.
1. Serve in bowls, float lemon slices on top and sprinkle with turnip leaves.

> Sanji: The deciding factor is the kombu broth!

### -24- Lakeshore Camp "Grilled Rock" Pork Stew

*Rich pork stew without roux — serves 4*

#### Ingredients

- 600 g pork shoulder, sliced 1.5 cm thick
- ½ tsp salt
- Dash black pepper
- 25 g flour
- 1 clove garlic, crushed
- 6 mushrooms, halved
- ½ onion (100 g), quartered
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 20 g butter (10 g + 10 g)
- 400 mL red wine
- 400 mL chicken stock (see Sanji's Chicken Stock)
- 2 bay leaves
- 30 g raisins
- 5 tbsp ketchup

#### Steps

1. Sprinkle pork with salt, pepper, and flour. Halve garlic and crush.
1. Heat oil in a pot over medium with garlic until fragrant; remove garlic. Add 10 g butter, brown pork on both sides with remaining flour.
1. Add red wine, boil off alcohol. Add chicken stock, the garlic, and bay leaves. Skim froth and remove bay leaves once liquid begins to film. Add raisins and ketchup. Simmer on low 40 minutes.
1. In a separate pan, fry mushrooms and onion in remaining 10 g butter. Add to the stew and simmer 20 more minutes. Season with salt.

### -26- Absalom's Croquette

*Full of beef! Potato croquettes — makes 10*

#### Ingredients

- 4 potatoes (600 g)
- 200 g beef, cut into 2 cm pieces
- ½ onion (100 g), thinly sliced
- ½ tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 tsp salt
- Dash pepper
- 20 g butter
- 1–2 eggs
- Flour, panko, and frying oil as needed
- Shredded cabbage and Worcestershire sauce to serve

#### Steps

1. Steam potatoes ~40 minutes until soft.
1. Fry beef in oil over medium. Add onion and cook until translucent. Season with ½ tsp salt and pepper. Set aside.
1. Peel and mash potatoes with butter and ½ tsp salt. Mix in the beef and onion. Spread out and cool.
1. Divide into 10 flat ovals. Coat in flour, beaten egg, then panko. Fry in 180°C oil for 3 minutes.
1. Dab with paper towel. Serve with shredded cabbage and sauce.

### -76- Mock Town Cherry Pie

*Makes 1 pie, 21 cm diameter*

#### Ingredients

**Dough:**
- 60 g weak flour
- 60 g strong flour
- 80 g salted butter, cut into 1 cm cubes
- 10 g milk
- ½ egg
- 15 g granulated sugar

**Custard cream:**
- 2 egg yolks
- 65 g granulated sugar
- 10 g cornstarch
- 10 g weak flour
- 230 mL milk
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- 20 g salted butter
- 18 American cherries

#### Steps

1. Chill flour and butter separately. Mix milk, egg, and sugar; chill. Cut butter into flour with a pastry cutter. Add egg mixture and mix with spatula. Wrap and refrigerate 30–60 minutes.
1. Roll out dough on parchment to fit pie pan. Press into pan, trim edges, prick base with a fork. Line with parchment and weights.
1. Bake at 200°C for 15 minutes. Remove weights, reduce to 180°C, bake 15 more minutes. Cool on a rack.
1. Custard: mix egg yolks, sugar, cornstarch, flour, and milk in a pot. Heat, stirring, until thickened. Remove from heat, add butter and lemon juice, mix smooth. Cool, then pour into pie shell.
1. Halve and de-stone cherries. Arrange on custard. Refrigerate until set.

**Caramel sauce:**
- 3 tbsp sugar

#### Steps
